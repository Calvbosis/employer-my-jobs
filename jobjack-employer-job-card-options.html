Job Card — Layout Options

Three directions for the same card, rendered against the same four jobs (a full-funnel role, a fresh waitlist duplicate, an all-zero pending posting, and a completed bulk-hire role) so the layouts can be judged against real, awkward data — not just the tidy case. A keeps the current 3-per-row structure; E and F vary the grid density itself — 2-per-row and a full-width stacked list.

Recommendation

On density (A/E vs. F): A and E both trade total jobs-per-screen for a more comfortable read per card — reasonable if this page turns out to be more "review a handful of jobs closely" than "scan hundreds." F is the other extreme: a true list, most jobs visible per screen, closest to a spreadsheet — best if the real usage pattern is scanning large job counts fast, at the cost of losing the card's visual identity (no clear title/chip hierarchy, everything reads as one dense row). Given branches can range up to 20+, and the employer's stated need was fast disambiguation across volume, F is worth serious consideration over the default 3-per-row grid — but it's a genuinely different product feel, so this should be a deliberate choice, not a default.
